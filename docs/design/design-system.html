Ayni Sanctuary

Seven wings, one ecosystem, rooted in Calca. Paper, ink, and seven earthy hues. Nothing decorative.

Color

Two surfaces, two inks, seven hues

The page is paper. Text is ink. Everything else is accent, and accents are rare. Seven hues because there are seven wings, and seven wings because there are seven chakras: the energy centers this ecosystem is built around. Each hue is earthy enough to sit beside the next without competing.

Space

Space is strategy

Eight stops on a 4px base. Not because eight is sacred. Because fewer than eight is too tight for editorial work, and more than eight invites indecision. Every gap on this page is one of these eight.
